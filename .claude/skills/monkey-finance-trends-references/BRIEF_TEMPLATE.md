### 🎬 Hook (First 30 Seconds)

**Opening line:**
> "[Write the exact first sentence — must create a pattern interrupt or make a bold claim]"

**Hook structure:**
1. Bold claim or shocking stat to grab attention
2. Relate it to the viewer's situation ("If you've got a savings account in the UK right now...")
3. Promise what they'll learn by the end ("By the end of this video you'll know exactly how to...")
4. Tease the payoff without giving it away

### 📐 Video Structure

Recommended length: **10–12 minutes**

| Section | Content | Approx time |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | Bold opener, viewer promise | 0:00–0:45 |
| Problem framing | Why this topic matters to them personally | 0:45–2:00 |
| Core explanation | Main content, broken into clear sections | 2:00–8:30 |
| Key takeaways | Summarise the 3–5 main points | 8:30–10:00 |
| CTA | Subscribe prompt, related video link | 10:00–11:00 |
