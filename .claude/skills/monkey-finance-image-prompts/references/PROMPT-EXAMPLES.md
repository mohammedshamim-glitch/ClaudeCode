# PROMPT-EXAMPLES.md — 15 Annotated Example Prompts

All examples are drawn from the Pension IHT Double Tax Bomb script. Each is annotated to explain the decisions made. Use these as benchmarks when writing your own prompts.

---

## Example 01 — Type A (Hook / Brand Open)
**Scene context:** Opening hook — government changing pension rules, urgency

```
2D colourful whiteboard animation style. Clean white background. A 
confident monkey in a bright green suit stands on the right side of 
the canvas, one arm outstretched pointing firmly to the left, 
expression serious and urgent. On the left, bold red hand-drawn 
marker text in large block capitals: "YOUR PENSION WILL BE TAXED 
WHEN YOU DIE". Below it in slightly smaller black marker: "Here's 
what to do." A thick red explosion starburst drawn behind the 
headline text, adding urgency. The composition is weighted left 
(content) and right (character), with clear breathing room between 
them. Bold colourful hand-drawn illustration, 16:9 widescreen aspect 
ratio, landscape composition.
```

**Why it works:**
- ✅ Green suit monkey — correct for Scene 01
- ✅ Monkey faces the content (points left toward text)
- ✅ Red used for danger/urgency, black for secondary text
- ✅ Starburst adds energy without clutter
- ✅ No logo, no whiteboard object

## Example 02 — Type G (Narrative / Story)
**Scene context:** "Picture this — 30 years paying into a pension, then rules change"

```
2D colourful whiteboard animation style. Clean white background. A 
simple horizontal timeline drawn in black marker spans the canvas. 
At the left end, a small hand-drawn figure drops a coin labeled "£" 
into a growing stack. Tick marks appear along the line — each 
labeled in small black marker: "Year 1", "Year 10", "Year 20", 
"Year 30". The pension stack grows taller at each mark, coloured 
green. At the "2027" mark on the far right, a thick red X slashes 
through the timeline and the green stack cracks with jagged red 
lines. Red lightning bolt icons strike it. Black marker text above 
the X: "Rules Change." The emotional shift from green (growth) to 
red (damage) tells the whole story. Bold colourful hand-drawn 
illustration, 16:9 widescreen aspect ratio, landscape composition.
```

**Why it works:**
- ✅ No monkey — the timeline narrative is the story
- ✅ Green-to-red colour shift mirrors the emotional arc
- ✅ Simple iconic elements (coin, stack, lightning) not complex illustrations
- ✅ "2027" appears clearly as a marker

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| "A monkey stands at a whiteboard" | "A monkey stands on the canvas" — no whiteboard object |
| Green suit monkey mid-video | Reserve for Scene 01 and final scene only |
| Forgetting to write the stat on canvas | Every number in narration MUST be in the prompt as drawn text |
| Two consecutive stat reveal scenes | Vary with a reaction or story scene in between |
| "A Monkey Finance logo appears" | Never. No logos. Ever. |
| Over-cluttered scene (5+ elements) | Maximum 4 visual elements per scene |
| Generic expression ("the monkey looks concerned") | Specific expression ("furrowed brow, one hand on chin, leaning forward") |
