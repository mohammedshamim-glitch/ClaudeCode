# SEO.md — Title Engineering, Description, Tags, Hashtags & Chapters

## The SEO Hierarchy

YouTube's algorithm ranks on two axes simultaneously:
1. **Relevance** — does your metadata match what people search for?
2. **Performance** — do people click it, watch it, and engage with it?

You need both. A perfectly SEO'd title nobody clicks is invisible. A clickbait title with 30-second retention gets penalised. The goal is **keyword-matched metadata that also compels a human to click and stay.**

Target CTR benchmarks:
- **2–4%** — below average, packaging needs work
- **4–6%** — solid, you're in the game
- **6–10%** — excellent, algorithm starts pushing hard
- **10%+** — viral territory for the finance niche

## Title Engineering

### The Rules

**Rule 1: Primary keyword first, within 40 characters**
YouTube truncates titles on mobile at ~40 characters in some views. The primary keyword must appear before the cut.

✅ `Pension Tax Changes 2027 — What You Must Do Now`
❌ `Here's Everything You Need to Know About the Upcoming Pension Tax Changes`

**Rule 2: Under 60 characters total**
The sweet spot. Forces clarity. Prevents mobile truncation. The best finance titles are 8–12 words.

**Rule 3: One keyword phrase, one benefit or hook**
Don't try to pack in three ideas. One keyword. One reason to click. That's it.

**Rule 4: Use digits, not words**
`5 Things` beats `Five Things`. Numbers are visual anchors. They signal structure and specificity.

**Rule 5: Add a year when the topic is time-sensitive**
`Best Cash ISA Rates 2026` ranks higher than `Best Cash ISA Rates` — the year signals freshness.

**Rule 6: Front-load the value, add curiosity or urgency at the end**
`[Keyword + what they get] — [emotional hook]`
> `Mortgage Rates UK 2026 — Should You Fix Now or Wait?`

### Title Formula Library

Use these as templates. Swap in the relevant keyword and hook.

**Formula 1: Keyword + Question**
`[Topic] — [Question viewers are asking]`
> `Cash ISA vs Stocks ISA — Which One Actually Wins?`

**Formula 2: Keyword + Warning**
`[Topic] — [What they're about to lose/miss]`
> `Pension Tax 2027 — The Rule Change Most People Are Missing`

**Formula 3: Keyword + Number + Outcome**
`[Number] [Topic] [Tips/Things/Steps] That [Outcome]`
> `5 Pension Mistakes That Could Cost Your Family Thousands`

**Formula 4: Keyword + Counterintuitive**
`Why [Common Belief About Topic] Is Wrong`
> `Why Fixing Your Mortgage Right Now Could Be a Mistake`

**Formula 5: Keyword + Urgency**
`[Topic] — Do This Before [Deadline]`
> `ISA Allowance 2026 — Do This Before April Ends`

**Formula 6: Keyword + Revelation**
`The Truth About [Topic] Nobody Tells You`
> `The Truth About UK Pensions Most Financial Advisers Hide`

**Formula 7: Keyword + Specificity**
`How to [Specific Outcome] in [Timeframe/Context]`
> `How to Protect Your Pension From the 2027 Tax Grab`

### Alt Title Strategy

Always produce 3 title variants targeting different emotional hooks:
- **Primary** — SEO-first, keyword at front, clear value
- **Alt A** — emotion-forward, fear or curiosity dominant
- **Alt B** — benefit-forward, aspiration or relief dominant

---

### YouTube A/B Title Testing — Mandatory Step

Producing 3 title variants is worthless unless you actually test them. Every video must go through YouTube Studio's **Test & Compare** feature.

**How to run the test (step by step):**

1. Upload the video and publish with the **Primary** title
2. Go to **YouTube Studio → Content → click the video**
3. Click **"Test & Compare"** in the left menu (under Details)
4. Add **Alt A** as Test B, and **Alt B** as Test C
5. Set test duration: **minimum 7 days** — shorter tests produce unreliable results
6. Let YouTube split impressions across all three titles automatically

**What to measure:**
- **Click-through rate (CTR)** — the primary metric. Which title got more clicks per impression?
- **Watch time per viewer** — a title that attracts the wrong audience drops this
- **Views** — not the primary metric; CTR matters more at early stage

**Decision rule:**
- If one variant wins by **0.5%+ CTR** → switch permanently to that title
- If results are within 0.2% → keep Primary (SEO-first is safer long-term)
- If all three underperform (CTR under 3%) → the packaging needs a full rethink — revisit thumbnail and title together

**Important:** YouTube's Test & Compare is only available on videos with enough impressions to generate statistically meaningful results. For a newer channel, let the video run 14 days before drawing conclusions.

**Record results in the trend report for the next video cycle** — which emotional hook is outperforming for your audience? Fear/warning, aspiration, or curiosity? This pattern builds over time and tells you exactly how to package future videos.

## Description Architecture

### The 3-Zone Structure

**Zone 1: The Hook (lines 1–2, ~60 words)**
This is what appears BEFORE "Show More." It must work as a standalone hook. Write it for a human, not a crawler.

- Line 1: State the core value proposition directly. Include primary keyword naturally.
- Line 2: Expand the promise. Add urgency or specificity.

> `Pensions are about to be taxed at death for the first time — and most UK savers have no idea it's coming. In this video, we break down exactly what's changing, who it affects, and the steps you can take right now.`

**Zone 2: The Body (150–300 words)**
This is where YouTube's algorithm reads for keyword signals. Write naturally — no stuffing. Cover:
- What the video covers (2–4 bullet points using keywords)
- Key secondary keywords woven into sentences
- A soft CTA: "Subscribe for weekly UK finance explained in plain English"

**Zone 3: The Links & Hashtags (bottom)**
- 3–5 internal links (related videos or playlists)
- 1 channel link
- 3–5 hashtags on the final line

## Tags

### The Tag Pyramid

Structure tags from broad → specific → long-tail. 10–12 tags total. Only the first 15 count; stop at 12.

**Tier 1 — Broad (2–3 tags):** Category-level terms
> `personal finance`, `UK money`, `finance explained`

**Tier 2 — Topic (3–4 tags):** The specific topic
> `pension tax`, `inheritance tax pension`, `SIPP death benefit`

**Tier 3 — Specific (2–3 tags):** Exact phrases people search
> `pension IHT 2027`, `pension inheritance tax changes UK`

**Tier 4 — Long-tail (2 tags):** Questions or full phrases
> `what happens to my pension when I die UK`, `pension death tax explained`

### Rules
- Never use irrelevant tags to chase traffic — YouTube penalises this
- Include the primary keyword as a standalone tag
- Include the channel name as one tag: `Monkey Finance`
- Include `UK` or `United Kingdom` in at least one tag
- Use the current year in at least one tag

## Hashtags

Use 3–5 hashtags. Place them at the very end of the description (not in the title).

YouTube displays the top 3 hashtags above the video title — choose the first 3 carefully.

**Format:** `#UKPersonalFinance #PensionTax #MonkeyFinance`

**Rules:**
- First hashtag: broad niche (`#PersonalFinanceUK` or `#UKFinance`)
- Second hashtag: specific topic (`#PensionTax` or `#MortgageRates`)
- Third hashtag: channel brand (`#MonkeyFinance`)
- Fourth/fifth: optional — additional topic or trending term

## Chapter Timestamps

Chapters massively improve watch time, SEO (chapters appear in Google search), and user experience. Always include them.

**Format:**
```
0:00 Introduction
0:45 [Section name]
2:30 [Section name]
5:00 [Section name — this is the core content]
8:30 Key Takeaways
10:00 What To Do Next
```

**Rules:**
- First timestamp must be `0:00`
- Minimum 3 chapters for YouTube to activate the feature
- Chapter names should include keywords where natural
- The core content chapter title should be the most specific/clickable
- Keep chapter names under 40 characters
