# THUMBNAIL.md — Thumbnail Design System & Brief Format

## Why Thumbnails Win or Lose Channels

CTR is the algorithm's first verdict on your packaging. Every percentage point of CTR improvement compounds:
- Videos with custom thumbnails get 60–70% higher CTR than auto-generated ones
- Consistent thumbnail branding gives established channels 15–20% higher CTR from returning viewers
- Thumbnails with emotional expression get 20–30% higher CTR than neutral ones
- Under 12 characters of text on a thumbnail significantly outperforms text-heavy designs

The thumbnail and title work as a unit. They must tell the same story, not duplicate each other.

**The thumbnail's job:** stop the scroll and create enough curiosity/urgency to earn a click — without revealing so much that there's nothing to watch.

## The Monkey Finance Design System

### Visual Identity — v2 (bold black & red, full-bleed)
> **This supersedes the old clean-white whiteboard thumbnails.** The killer was **empty white space** (small elements floating in lots of exposed white), NOT white itself — Wealth Logic's own thumbnails sit on a white/light background, but it's almost entirely COVERED by big imagery and huge text. The new standard copies that proven formula: fill the frame, bold black + red, one big character, rich imagery, a scary number and/or a red arrow.
- **Style:** Bold 2D cartoon illustration that **FILLS THE FRAME** — large imagery + huge text cover almost all of it, so very little background shows. A plain white/light background is fine **as long as it's mostly covered**. No borders, no frames, and never small elements stranded in empty white.
- **Character:** Monkey in a **green suit — drawn BIG** (upper-body close-up, ~40% of the frame) with an exaggerated expression AND a gesture. **VARY the expression/pose every thumbnail — do NOT default to the two-handed shrug every time** (they blur together). Pick the emotion that fits THIS video from the Expression & Action guide below: thoughtful chin-stroke, shocked jaw-drop + stop hand, excited fist-pump, stern finger-point, curious head-tilt, worried hands-to-face, etc. Our mascot = their suited man.
- **Text:** **Bold BLACK + RED only.** Black = the setup, **RED = the danger word / scary number**. 3–4 words MAX, huge, thick. Text must ADD a hook, never just repeat the title.
- **Stakes:** a **thick red crashing arrow** and/or a **big number**, plus one real object (coin, £ note, product).
- **No logos. No channel name. No watermarks.**

### Marker Colour Strategy by Emotion

| Emotional trigger | Primary marker colour | Secondary | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fear / Warning | Thick red marker | Black header | Danger, urgency — red reads instantly |
| Shock / Surprise | Bold red + yellow | Black header | Alarm + attention — punchy combination |
| Aspiration / Wealth | Thick green marker | Black header | Money, growth, positive momentum |
| Curiosity | Yellow question mark + blue | Black header | Mystery, energy, intrigue |
| Relief / Calm | Thick blue marker | Black header | Trust, clarity, reassurance |

### Text on the Whiteboard Rules
- **Max 5 words total** across all text on the whiteboard — 3 is better, 1–2 is best if the visual carries meaning
- **No full sentences** — bold fragments and phrases only
- **Use numbers** — visual anchors: `£34,000` in red marker stops the scroll. Words don't.
- **Largest text = the headline** — everything else is supporting detail
- **The 12-character rule:** Under 12 characters of text significantly outperforms text-heavy designs

### Composition Rules
- Monkey always has a clear sightline toward the key text/stat
- The viewer's eye should travel: monkey expression → key stat/text → supporting element
- Leave breathing room — never pack the whiteboard edge to edge
- The most important element gets the most whiteboard real estate

## Thumbnail Composition Framework

Every Monkey Finance thumbnail follows one of these three layouts. All use the whiteboard animation style.

### Layout A: Monkey + Stat
**The big number layout** — the stat is the hero, the monkey reacts to it.
```
[LARGE STAT in bold marker — left two-thirds]     [Monkey in green suit reacting — right third]
[2-3 word label below stat]
[Optional: hand-drawn icon next to stat]
[Headline text at top of whiteboard in black marker]
```
Best for: Tax percentages, rate changes, savings figures, shocking numbers

### Layout B: Monkey + Question/Hook
**The curiosity layout** — the question is on the whiteboard, monkey looks like they need the answer.
```
[HEADLINE QUESTION at top — bold black marker, coloured underline]
[Subtext below — slightly smaller, coloured marker]
[Optional: hand-drawn visual element — graph, icon]
[Large yellow question mark — prominent position]
[Monkey in green suit — centre-left, reacting to the question]
```
Best for: Decisions, counterintuitive takes, "should I?", "why?" topics

### Layout C: "X VS Y / WHICH SHOULD YOU BUY?" treasure-pile comparison (DEFAULT for every versus video)
**The proven comparison layout** — full-bleed, packed with rich imagery, character thinking in the middle. This is the Wealth Logic comparison formula; use it for every "X vs Y" episode.
```
TOP: "[A] VS [B]" in huge bold BLACK caps, and under it "WHICH SHOULD YOU BUY?" (or a sharp question) in huge bold RED caps — fills the full width.
CENTRE: Monkey in a green suit, upper body, hand on chin in a thinking pose, looking between the two sides.
LEFT: a BIG RICH PILE representing option A (e.g. gold bars + coins).
RIGHT: a BIG RICH, EQUALLY APPEALING PILE representing option B (e.g. silver bars + coins).
A big bold yellow question mark above/between the character.
Both sides look equally tempting. Frame filled, minimal visible background.
```
**NON-NEGOTIABLE — never spoil the verdict:** The thumbnail POSES the question, it does not answer it. Keep the curiosity gap — do NOT reveal which option wins. That means NO "winner" cues on a comparison thumbnail: no green-up on one side + red-down on the other, no tick vs cross, no "20% VAT / loser" tag. Both options must look equally appealing so the viewer has to click to find out. (Wealth Logic's own Gold vs Silver thumbnail gave nothing away — both sides looked good, just "WHICH SHOULD YOU BUY?") Save directional arrows/ticks/crosses for single-subject warning thumbnails, never for versus thumbnails.
Rich real-looking piles beat flat single icons — the imagery is half the click. The thumbnail does NOT need to match the whiteboard video style; its only job is the click.
Best for: gold vs silver, ISA vs pension, property vs stocks, any versus topic

## Emotional Trigger Selection

The thumbnail must lead with one dominant emotion. Match to the topic:

### 😨 Fear / Warning
**Visual signals:** Red background or red text, monkey with wide eyes or alarmed expression, exclamation marks, downward arrows
**Text examples:** `TAX TRAP`, `COST YOU`, `TOO LATE?`, `WARNING`
**When:** Tax changes, rule changes, pension IHT, rising rates

### 🤑 Aspiration / Wealth
**Visual signals:** Green/gold palette, upward arrow, money graphic, monkey with confident/happy expression
**Text examples:** `EARN MORE`, `FREE MONEY?`, `GROW YOUR PENSION`, `ISA WIN`
**When:** Investment guides, savings rate alerts, ISA season, compound interest

### 😮 Shock / Surprise
**Visual signals:** Yellow background, monkey with dropped jaw or double-take expression, bold contrast
**Text examples:** `NOBODY KNOWS`, `THEY LIED`, `THE TRUTH`, `WAIT WHAT`
**When:** Counterintuitive insights, bank exposés, hidden rules, surprising stats

### 🧐 Curiosity
**Visual signals:** Purple/teal, monkey with tilted-head curious expression, question mark, blurred or partial reveal
**Text examples:** `THE SECRET`, `DO THIS NOW`, `HIDDEN RULE`, `REALLY?`
**When:** Tips and tricks, underknown rules, "most people don't know" topics

### 😅 Relief / Urgency
**Visual signals:** Clean white/blue, monkey with relief or determined expression, clock or calendar visual cue
**Text examples:** `STILL TIME`, `DO THIS TODAY`, `BEFORE APRIL`, `EASY FIX`
**When:** Deadline-based actions, easy wins, damage control after bad news

## The Thumbnail-Title Relationship

The thumbnail and title are a package. They must work together without duplicating each other.

**Rule:** If the title already says it, the thumbnail should show it visually. If the thumbnail shows a number, the title shouldn't repeat it.

| Title says... | Thumbnail should... |
|---|---|
| "Your Pension Will Be Taxed When You Die" | Show the shocked monkey + `40% GONE` |
| "Should You Fix Your Mortgage Now?" | Show monkey at a crossroads visual + `FIX OR WAIT?` |
| "Cash ISA vs Stocks ISA" | Use split layout A vs B |
| "5 Pension Mistakes Costing You Thousands" | Show alarmed monkey + `PENSION TRAP` |

**The curiosity gap rule:** The thumbnail raises a question or creates tension. The title either deepens that tension OR provides just enough context to make the click feel safe. Together they create the compulsion to click.

## Common Thumbnail Mistakes (Finance / Whiteboard Style)

- **Too much text** — more than 5 words on the whiteboard, or text too small to read at mobile size
- **Neutral monkey expression** — expressionless or stiff. The emotion must read at a glance even at 120px
- **Digital-looking fonts** — the whiteboard text must look hand-drawn, not like a computer font was pasted on
- **Busy whiteboard** — more than 3 elements competing for attention. Keep it sparse and bold.
- **Misleading visuals** — a thumbnail that overpromises leads to high CTR + terrible retention. Algorithm penalises permanently.
- **Monkey disconnected from the whiteboard** — the monkey must be reacting TO the content, not just standing next to it
- **Low contrast elements** — everything on the whiteboard must be bold, thick, and instantly readable at thumbnail size
- **Forgetting the 12-character rule** — count your characters. Under 12 wins every time.
