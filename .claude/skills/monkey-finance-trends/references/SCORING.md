# SCORING.md — Opportunity Scoring Matrix

Each video idea is scored out of 5 across 5 dimensions. Maximum score: 25.

**Threshold:** Only proceed with ideas scoring **18+**. Ideas scoring 12–17 are "monitor" candidates. Drop anything under 12.

---

## Dimension 1: 🔍 Search Volume (0–5)

How many people are actively searching for this topic?

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 5 | Very high volume — primary keyword has massive search demand, topic appears in Google autocomplete, multiple high-view videos exist |
| 4 | High volume — clearly searched, decent competition exists, topic trends periodically |
| 3 | Moderate — niche but consistent search demand, appears in long-tail queries |
| 2 | Low — limited search data, mostly social/news driven rather than search-driven |
| 1 | Very low — emerging signal only, no established search demand yet |
| 0 | No evidence of search demand |

---

## Dimension 2: ⚔️ Competition Level (0–5)

*Lower competition = higher score* (inverted scale — we want easy wins)

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 5 | Very low — few videos exist, top results are old (2+ years), no big channels dominating |
| 4 | Low — some competition but smaller channels rank, refresh opportunity exists |
| 3 | Moderate — mix of big and small channels, specific angle could still win |
| 2 | High — dominated by 100k–1M channels, hard to break through without angle |
| 1 | Very high — top 5 results all from 1M+ channels, highly saturated |
| 0 | Impossible — dominated by YouTube giants (MoneySavingExpert, Martin Lewis, major banks) |

---

## Dimension 3: 📈 Trend Velocity (0–5)

Is this topic rising, peaking, or fading?

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 5 | Breaking / accelerating — in the news now, Google Trends spiking upward, Reddit buzzing |
| 4 | Rising — clear upward trajectory in last 30 days, growing interest signals |
| 3 | Steady — consistent interest, not growing fast but reliable traffic |
| 2 | Peaking — at or near peak, still viable but window is closing |
| 1 | Declining — past peak, interest falling |
| 0 | Dead — trend has passed, no meaningful interest remaining |

---

## Dimension 4: 💰 Monetisation Potential (0–5)

How valuable is this topic for revenue?

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 5 | Finance gold — high CPM topic (ISAs, pensions, investing, mortgages), strong affiliate potential (comparison sites, apps, platforms) |
| 4 | High CPM — finance-adjacent, good advertiser interest, some affiliate angle |
| 3 | Medium CPM — general money topic, moderate advertiser interest |
| 2 | Lower CPM — broad lifestyle/money topic, limited premium advertisers |
| 1 | Low CPM — entertainment-skewing, minimal advertiser value |
| 0 | No monetisation angle |

---

## Dimension 5: 🎯 Channel Fit (0–5)

How well does this suit Monkey Finance's format, tone, and audience?

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 5 | Perfect fit — UK-relevant, explainer-friendly, suits 10–12 min whiteboard format, relatable to 25–45 audience, plain English topic |
| 4 | Strong fit — slight adaptation needed but works well within our format |
| 3 | Decent fit — requires more work to make accessible, or partially UK-relevant |
| 2 | Weak fit — mostly US-focused, or too complex/niche for our audience |
| 1 | Poor fit — doesn't suit our tone or format without major rework |
| 0 | Not suitable — wrong niche, wrong audience, or contradicts channel identity |

## Scoring Summary Table

Use this to rank all shortlisted ideas:

```
| Idea | Search Vol | Competition | Trend Velocity | Monetisation | Channel Fit | TOTAL |
|------|-----------|-------------|----------------|--------------|-------------|-------|
|      |    /5     |     /5      |      /5        |     /5       |     /5      |  /25  |
```

**Decision guide:**
- **20–25** → Strong go. Prioritise immediately.
- **18–19** → Go. Solid opportunity with minor caveats.
- **15–17** → Conditional. Worth doing if nothing better scores higher.
- **12–14** → Monitor. Revisit in 2–4 weeks.
- **Under 12** → Drop. Don't waste production time.
